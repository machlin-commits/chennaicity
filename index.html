Your city. Your pace.

Drive the mapped curves of Anna Salai, the G.N. Chetty Road arm, Cathedral Road and Uthamar Gandhi Salai. The scene covers 500 m north, south, east and west of Gemini Circle. Keep left and use the ramps to change levels.

W / ↑ accelerates. S / ↓ slows, then reverses. A D / ← → steer. Space brakes. C cycles chase, bonnet and orbit; drag to rotate orbit. Tap Metro or press M to watch the train. R returns to the south ramp.

Road centre lines, building footprints and landmark positions come from embedded OpenStreetMap data. Building heights use mapped levels when available; unrecorded heights and facade details are approximate. No satellite textures or external models are loaded. The visible elevated metro is a reference-inspired addition; the actual local metro is underground. Boards use mapped names where available; numbered Building labels identify unnamed footprints.
